Why this comes first

Most families have a tree that is part record and part recollection. The two are not the same, and only one of them belongs in a book that later generations will rely on. The Research Review sorts them out before anyone spends money on a full project.

It also answers the question every serious inquiry starts with, which is what this will actually cost. I do not quote a book or a research project until I have seen what the records hold. Once I have, I give you a firm number rather than a range.
